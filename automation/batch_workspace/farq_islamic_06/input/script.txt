Ye hai Shirk aur ye hai Bidah, aakhir isme farq kya hai? Aksar log Shirk aur Bidah ko ek hi burai samajhte hain, lekin inme bada farq hai. Shirk Allah ke saath kisi ko shareek karna hai, yani ibadat mein ghair Allah ko shamil karna. Lekin Bidah Deen mein aisi nai cheez daalna hai jiske liye koi saboot na ho, chahe niyat achi bhi ho. Shirk tawheed ko tod deta hai, jabke Bidah sunnat ke khilaf naya tariqa hai. Mazeed videos ke liye follow karein.
