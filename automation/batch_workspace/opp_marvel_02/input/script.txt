This is Wolverine. This is Deadpool. So what's the difference? Most people think Wolverine and Deadpool have the exact same healing factor. They don't. Wolverine's natural mutant healing factor constantly fights off his heavy Adamantium skeletal poisoning while repairing cellular damage. But Deadpool's artificial healing factor was cloned from Wolverine but mutated by his aggressive cancer, regenerating cells so fast his body constantly replaces itself. Wolverine heals to survive his own bones, while Deadpool heals to stay alive. Follow for more.
