HARAM aur MAKRUH mein sabse bada farq kya hai? HARAM shari'at ka qati aur lazmi mana karda amal hai jise karne par gunah aur azab hai. Jabke MAKRUH napasandida amal hai jise chhorne par sawab hai lekin karne par azab nahi. HARAM qati gunah hai, jabke MAKRUH napasandida amal. Aise hi Islamic comparisons ke liye follow karein.
