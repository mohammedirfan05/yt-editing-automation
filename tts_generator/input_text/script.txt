This is the MCU. This is Marvel Comics. So, what's the difference? The MCU is Marvel's shared film and TV universe, while Marvel Comics is the original universe where those stories began. This is the DCEU. This is the DCU. So, what's the difference? The DCEU was DC's previous shared film universe, while the DCU is the rebooted universe led by James Gunn and Peter Safran. This is canon. This is continuity. So, what's the difference? Canon is what officially happened in a story, while continuity is how those events stay consistent.
