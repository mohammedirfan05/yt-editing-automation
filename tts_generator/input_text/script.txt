This is the Mangekyō Sharingan.

This is the Rinnegan.

So, what's the difference?

Most people think they're the same.

They're not.

Here's the easiest way to remember it.

The Mangekyō Sharingan gives different powers to different users.

One gets Kamui.

Another gets Amaterasu.

The Rinnegan is different.

Everyone gets the same god-like abilities.

Things like pulling enemies, pushing them away, and the Six Paths powers.

The Mangekyō Sharingan gives unique powers, while the Rinnegan gives shared god-like powers.
